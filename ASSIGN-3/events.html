What each event involves

Code Sprint

A 3-hour competitive programming contest. Solve algorithm problems in teams of two, ranked on correctness and speed.

RoboRace

Build a line-following robot and race it on a timed track. Bring your own robot or use a kit supplied by the organizers.

Hack the Campus

A 6-hour hackathon where teams of up to four design an app or tool that solves a real problem found on campus.

UI Showdown

A live design challenge. Redesign a poorly built interface in 90 minutes, then present your choices to the judges.

Tech Quiz

A multi-round quiz on technology, computing history and current trends, played in teams of three.

Project Expo

Showcase a finished project to visitors and industry guests. Open to every department. Free entry for all participants.
